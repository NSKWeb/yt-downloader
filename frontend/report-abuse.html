Report Abuse

Last updated: 15 October 2026

This page is a template provided with the source code. Have a qualified lawyer review and adapt it for your jurisdiction and your operation before you rely on it or publish it as your own policy.

What to report

  • Use of the service to infringe copyright at scale.
  • Use of the service to harass, stalk or threaten a person.
  • Attempts to attack, overload or breach the service.
  • Any use involving unlawful material.

What to include

  • What the problem is, described plainly.
  • The URLs, timestamps or job identifiers involved, where you have them.
  • Any evidence you can share, such as screenshots or logs.
  • How to reach you, if you want a reply.

Where to send it

Use the contact page and mark the message clearly as an abuse report, so it is routed correctly.

Copyright matters

Formal copyright complaints should follow the procedure on the DMCA page instead, which sets out the information a valid notice must contain.

Handling

We review reports as promptly as we can. Depending on what we find, we may rate limit, block or otherwise restrict access, and we may pass information to the relevant authorities where the law requires or permits it.

Good faith

Please report in good faith. Repeatedly filing reports you know to be false may itself be treated as abuse.
