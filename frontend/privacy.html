Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

This policy explains what information yt-downloader ("we", "us") collects when you use this website, why we collect it, and what control you have over it.

1. Information we collect

Information you provide

When you submit a download request we process the URL you enter and the format options you choose. We do not require an account and we do not ask for your name or email address to use the downloader.

Information collected automatically

Like most web servers, ours records standard request data: IP address, browser user agent, referring page, and timestamps. We use this for security, abuse prevention, and capacity planning. Server logs are retained for a limited period and then deleted.

2. How we use information

  • To carry out the download you requested
  • To detect and prevent abuse, fraud, and attacks on our infrastructure
  • To diagnose faults and understand aggregate usage
  • To comply with legal obligations

3. Cookies and advertising

This site uses cookies and similar technologies. If advertising is enabled on this site, third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet.
  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings at google.com/settings/ads.
  • You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at www.aboutads.info.
  • For more information about how Google uses data from sites that use its services, see Google's partner sites notice.

4. Third-party vendors

We may work with third-party advertising networks and analytics providers. These vendors may set their own cookies and collect data under their own privacy policies. We do not control those cookies. Where we use additional networks, they will be listed here together with their opt-out links.

5. Legal bases and your rights

Where the GDPR or similar law applies, we process server log data on the basis of our legitimate interest in keeping the service secure and available, and we process download requests to perform the service you asked for. Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your personal data, and to object to processing or request portability. Contact us to exercise any of these rights.

If you are in California, you may have rights under the CCPA/CPRA, including the right to know what personal information is collected and the right to request deletion. We do not sell personal information.

6. Data retention

Downloaded files are stored temporarily and deleted automatically after a short period. We do not keep a permanent copy of the media you download. Server logs are kept only as long as needed for the purposes described above.

7. Children's privacy

This service is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical measures to protect the service and the data we handle. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected in the "last updated" date above.
