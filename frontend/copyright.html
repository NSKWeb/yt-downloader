Respect for rights holders

This service exists to help people save content they are entitled to save. It is not a tool for redistributing other people's work, and using it that way is prohibited by the terms of service.

What the service does and does not do

The service fetches media from a URL you supply, on demand, for you. It does not host a library of media, does not provide a search index, and does not publish or distribute anything to third parties.

Your obligations

  • Only download content you own or are licensed or permitted to save.
  • Respect the terms of service of the platform you are downloading from.
  • Do not redistribute downloaded content in breach of copyright.
  • Do not remove or alter rights-management information.

Reporting infringement

Copyright complaints follow the procedure on the DMCA page. If your concern is not a formal copyright notice, the report abuse page is the right route.

Platform terms

The platforms this tool supports each have their own terms and copyright policies. Your agreement with those platforms is between you and them, and this service does not alter it.
