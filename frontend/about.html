About Us

Last updated: 15 October 2026

yt-downloader is a small, focused web tool. It takes a YouTube link, fetches the media on our server, and gives you the finished file. There is no account to create, no software to install, and no browser extension to trust.

What it offers

  • Video downloads from 360p up to 4320p (8K), where the source provides it
  • Audio extraction in MP3, M4A, WAV, FLAC, OPUS, AAC, and OGG
  • Multi-connection fetching, so large files finish quickly
  • Background jobs, so a long download survives a closed tab
  • A clean interface that works on phones as well as desktops

How it works

When you submit a link, our API validates it and places a job on a queue. A worker process picks up the job, downloads the video and audio streams, muxes them with FFmpeg, and writes the result to storage. Your browser polls for progress and downloads the file when it is ready. Files are deleted automatically after a short period.
