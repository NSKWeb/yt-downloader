Our position

We respect intellectual property rights. This site does not host, index, or permanently store media. It is a transient tool: files pass through temporary storage and are deleted automatically. We do not operate a library of downloadable content.

Filing a takedown notice

If you believe your copyrighted work is being infringed through this service, send a written notice to dmca@example.com including all of the following:

  1. A physical or electronic signature of the copyright owner or a person authorised to act on their behalf.
  2. Identification of the copyrighted work claimed to be infringed.
  3. Identification of the material claimed to be infringing, with enough detail for us to locate it (a specific URL, not a general description).
  4. Your contact details: full name, address, telephone number, and email.
  5. A statement that you have a good-faith belief the disputed use is not authorised by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
  6. A statement, made under penalty of perjury, that the information in your notice is accurate and that you are the copyright owner or are authorised to act on the owner's behalf.

What happens next

We review complete notices and act on valid ones promptly. Because this service does not host content, action typically means blocking a specific URL or restricting access to the tool, rather than removing a hosted file. We may contact the person responsible for the submission.

Counter-notice

If you believe material was removed or blocked in error, you may send a counter-notice to the same address with your contact details, identification of the material, and a statement under penalty of perjury that you have a good-faith belief the material was removed by mistake or misidentification.

Repeat infringers

We may restrict or block access for anyone who repeatedly submits infringing material.

Misrepresentation

Under applicable law, you may be liable for damages if you knowingly make a material misrepresentation in a takedown notice or counter-notice.
