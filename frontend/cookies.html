Summary

The downloader does not require cookies to work. It does not set advertising or analytics cookies, and it does not track you across sites.

What may be stored

  • Strictly necessary. If a deployment places the service behind authentication or a load balancer, a session cookie may be set by that layer. It is required for the service to function.
  • Local storage. The interface may keep a small amount of state in your browser, such as your last chosen format, so you do not have to reselect it. This stays on your device and is not transmitted to us.

What we do not use

  • No advertising or retargeting cookies.
  • No third-party analytics cookies.
  • No cross-site tracking pixels.

Managing cookies

You can clear or block cookies and local storage in your browser settings. Blocking strictly necessary cookies may prevent parts of the service from working.
