Get in touch

For bug reports, feature requests and general questions, open an issue on the project's GitHub repository. That is the fastest route and keeps the discussion public and searchable.

Copyright and takedown notices

If you believe content accessible through this service infringes your rights, see the DMCA page for the notice requirements and where to send them. Notices sent through the general contact route may take longer to reach the right person.

Abuse reports

To report misuse of this service, see the report abuse page.

What to include

  • What you expected to happen, and what happened instead.
  • The URL you were downloading, if it is safe to share it.
  • Any error message, exactly as it appeared.
  • Your browser and operating system, for interface problems.

Response times

This is a volunteer-maintained project. We aim to acknowledge reports promptly but cannot promise a fixed response time.
