Frequently Asked Questions

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Is it free?

Yes. There is no account and no payment.

What qualities can I download?

Any quality the source provides, from 360p up to 4320p (8K). If a video was only ever uploaded at 1080p, requesting 4K will fall back to the highest available. Choose "Best" to always take the top stream.

Which audio formats are supported?

MP3, M4A, WAV, FLAC, OPUS, AAC, and OGG.

Why does my download fail?

Common causes, in rough order of frequency:

  • The video is private, deleted, or region-locked.
  • The link is a playlist or channel URL. Single videos only.
  • The URL is not a YouTube link.
  • The server is rate-limited. Wait a few minutes and retry.

How long are files kept?

Files are temporary. They are deleted automatically a short time after the download completes. Save the file to your device promptly.

Can I download a whole playlist?

Not currently. One video per request.

Is this legal?

Downloading is legal for content you have the right to save: your own uploads, permissively licensed works, public-domain material, or anything you have permission to copy. Downloading copyrighted material you do not have rights to is your responsibility, and in many jurisdictions it is unlawful. See our Terms and Disclaimer.

Do you keep a copy of what I download?

No permanent copy. Files are transient and deleted automatically.

How do I report a copyright issue?

Follow the process on our DMCA page.
